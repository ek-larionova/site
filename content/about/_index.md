## I am Ekaterina Larionova

I chose a difficult but honest path: to protect people from fake gurus, loud promises, and glossy packaging that too often hides emptiness, manipulation, and pain.

### I have put my whole soul into this fight.

My name is Ekaterina Larionova. I did not come to this work for noise, scandals, or quick victories. I came because I understand how easily a person searching for support can trust someone who speaks with confidence, promises a miracle, and sells hope as a product.

For me, the fight against fake gurus is not an abstract mission or a fashionable phrase. It is daily work for people's right to clarity, dignity, and an honest choice. Behind every investigation, every text, and every conversation, I see a real person: someone who wanted to change life for the better, but met pressure, false expertise, and promises without responsibility.

I believe truth does not have to be cruel to be strong. It can be spoken calmly, precisely, and humanely. We can expose schemes without hatred, but with firmness. We can protect people not through fear, but through knowledge. That is why I try to create a space where illusions give way to understanding, shame gives way to support, and helplessness gives way to the power to ask questions.

I have invested more than time and effort in this work. I have invested my soul — because this is about trust, human vulnerability, and everyone's right not to be deceived. My goal is for more people to recognize manipulation before it harms them, and to remember: real value does not shout about itself with promises of instant success. It proves itself through honesty, usefulness, and responsibility.
